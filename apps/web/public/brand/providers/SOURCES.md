# Provider logos

Official logos of Philippine banks and e-wallets, downloaded unmodified from Wikimedia Commons. They are
trademarks of their owners and are shown only to identify the provider of an account the user tracks by
hand. Faldo is not affiliated with or endorsed by these providers. Before a public release, check each
brand's logo usage guidelines.
